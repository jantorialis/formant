the basics

formant is a vocal synth that generates voice-like sounds using formant synthesis, the same idea behind early vocaloids and text-to-speech. it creates a buzzy tone and shapes it with filters to produce sounds, all entirely in your browser!

type something → parse → click a word → use the piano roll to set pitches → sing!

piano roll

the piano roll shows all syllables of the selected word as colored blocks. each column is a syllable, and the rows are notes (c3 at the bottom, b5 at the top). click any cell in a column to move that syllable to that pitch, it updates live! the active syllable is highlighted.

syllables

each word auto-splits into syllables. you can add more, remove them, or change the text label. every syllable is its own note + phoneme, so you can sing different pitches across one word!

vowels

a phoneme is the sound the syllable makes.

what is formant synthesis?

when you speak, your vocal cords buzz and your throat / mouth shapes into vowels by amplifying certain frequencies. those frequencies are called formants! this synth fakes the same process using the klattsch engine, which implements a parallel-formant synthesizer in your browser.
